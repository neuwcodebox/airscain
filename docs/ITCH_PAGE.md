## Description

**Build the network. Hold the city.**

AIRSCAIN is a real-time air defense strategy game about protecting an island city with the network you build. Place sensors, command links, weapons and support facilities; then use the tracks your network actually detects to guide its response. Every layer has limits, and every raid forces you to reconsider coverage, resources and risk.

- **Detect and connect.** Position radars and command assets so information reaches the batteries that need it.
- **Build layered defenses.** Combine missiles, guns and other systems to cover different ranges and threats.
- **Keep the network working.** Manage ammunition, power, repairs and reinforcements as attacks change.
- **Choose your operation.** Survive as long as you can in Sustained Operation, learn the systems through 13 Training missions, or experiment in Sandbox.

Each battlefield generates a new island and city. There is no fixed final wave: keep the city functioning for as long as you can.

Play in your browser or download the Windows x64 build. Keyboard and mouse required. English and Korean interfaces are available in Settings. This playable build remains in active development.

## Download and install instructions

**Browser:** Select “Run game” and use a keyboard and mouse.  
**Windows:** Download `Airscain-windows-x64.zip`, extract it, keep `Airscain.exe` and `Airscain.pck` in the same folder, then run `Airscain.exe`. No Godot installation is needed.  
**Controls:** WASD moves the camera; the mouse wheel zooms; middle-mouse drag rotates; left click selects or places; right click or Esc cancels placement. Pause and speed controls are at the top right.

**브라우저:** `Run game`을 누른 뒤 키보드·마우스로 플레이하세요.  
**Windows:** ZIP을 풀고 `Airscain.exe`와 `Airscain.pck`를 같은 폴더에 둔 채 실행하세요. Godot 설치는 필요 없습니다.
