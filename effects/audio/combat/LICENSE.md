# Combat audio license

All audio files in this directory were selected from Freesound under the
Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Universal license (CC0 1.0).

- Source: https://freesound.org/
- License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Attribution is not required.

The files are organized by their intended use. Their embedded Vorbis metadata
is preserved, including author information where it was present in the
download.

- `big_explosion_*.ogg`: city impacts
- `explosion_*.ogg`: missile and general combat explosions
- `long_missile_*.ogg`: long-range missile launches
- `missile_*.ogg`: standard missile launches
- `short_missile_*.ogg`: short-range missile launches
